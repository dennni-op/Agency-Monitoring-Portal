Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a full observability platform?

No. It's a focused reliability reporting layer for AI provider uptime, latency, and weekly operational visibility. It sits alongside your existing stack without requiring architectural changes.

Do I need to change my product architecture?

No. The system monitors the providers and models you already use. It runs scheduled health checks independently and does not require integration into your app code.

Can this work for agencies with multiple clients?

Yes. The multi-client structure is one of our strongest use cases, especially for white-label weekly reporting and per-client isolation.

How fast can this be live?

Most pilots can be live within 2-4 business days once you provide provider API keys and database access.

What if I only use one provider?

This works well for single-provider setups too. Many teams use this to track a single provider's reliability trends over time, catch degradation early, and prove uptime to clients.

Can I see a sample report before committing?

Absolutely. We can share example weekly reports and walk you through how your specific provider mix would be tracked and reported.

What's included in the pilot?

Setup, configuration for your providers, two weeks of live monitoring, your first weekly report, and a review call to discuss findings and next steps.

Can I cancel anytime after the pilot?

Yes. After the pilot, you can choose to continue with a monthly plan or stop at any time. No long-term contracts.
