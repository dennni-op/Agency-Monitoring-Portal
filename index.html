Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a full observability platform?

No. It's a focused reliability reporting layer for AI provider uptime and latency. It sits alongside your existing stack without requiring architectural changes.

Do I need to change my product architecture?

No. The system monitors the providers and models you already use. It runs scheduled health checks independently with no code integration required.

Can this work for agencies with multiple clients?

Yes. Multi-client isolation and white-label reporting is one of our strongest use cases for agencies managing AI features for multiple customers.

How fast can this be live?

Most pilots go live within 2-4 business days once you provide provider API keys and database access.

What if I only use one provider?

This works well for single-provider setups. Many teams use it to track reliability trends, catch degradation early, and prove uptime to clients.

Can I see a sample report before committing?

Absolutely. We can share example weekly reports and walk you through how your specific provider mix would be tracked and reported.

What's included in the pilot?

Setup, configuration for your providers, two weeks of live monitoring, your first weekly report, and a review call to discuss findings.

Can I cancel anytime after the pilot?

Yes. After the pilot, you can continue with a monthly plan or stop at any time. No long-term contracts.
